Cong-Thanh Vu, Ph.D.

Director, Robotics and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (RASL)
Assistant Professor, Department of Mechatronics Engineering, HAUI, Vietnam
Email: vuthanh.cdt@gmail.com
Website: vuthanhcdt.github.io
Google Scholar: Cong-Thanh Vu
Location: Hanoi, Vietnam

Research Interests

  • Human–Robot Interaction & Social Robotics: Adaptive companionship, proactive assistance, human-aware mobile robot navigation, and group following.
  • Field & Legged Robotics: Agricultural mobile robots, adaptive pesticide spraying, passive dynamic walking, and humanoid locomotion.
  • Robot Learning & AI: Deep reinforcement learning, continual learning for tracking, and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) multimodal models.

Education

  • Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering, National Cheng Kung University (NCKU), Tainan, Taiwan (Mar. 2022 – Jun. 2026).
  • M.S. in Mechatronics Engineering, Hanoi University of Industry (HAUI), Hanoi, Vietnam (Aug. 2019 – Jun. 2021).
  • B.S. in Mechatronics Engineering, Hanoi University of Industry (HAUI), Hanoi, Vietnam (Aug. 2015 – Jun. 2019).

Academic & Professional Experience

  • Assistant Professor & Lab Director (RASL), Department of Mechatronics Engineering, School of Mechanical and Automotive Engineering (SMAE), Hanoi University of Industry (HAUI), Hanoi, Vietnam (Aug. 2026 – Present).
  • Graduate Research Assistant, Department of Mechanical Engineering, National Cheng Kung University (NCKU), Tainan, Taiwan (Mar. 2022 – Jun. 2026).
  • Research Assistant, Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Economics - Technology for Industries (UNETI), Hanoi, Vietnam (Aug. 2021 – Feb. 2022).
