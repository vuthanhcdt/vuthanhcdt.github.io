Welcome to RASL HAUI!

  • Robotics and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (RASL) in the Department of Mechatronics Engineering, School of Mechanical and Automotive Engineering (SMAE) at Hanoi University of Industry (HAUI) is directed by Cong-Thanh Vu.
  • Our Vision "Technology is a bridge connecting intelligence and humanity to shape a better future" — RASL focuses on research in intelligent robotics and autonomous systems, spanning human–robot interaction, legged locomotion, agricultural robotics, social robots, and autonomous control. By integrating robotics, AI, and control theory, RASL aims to develop practical solutions for real-world applications in industry, agriculture, and service robotics.

Core Research Topics

Human–Robot Interaction

Adaptive companionship, proactive assistance, and intuitive multimodal communication between humans and service/companion robots.

Human-Aware Navigation

Socially compliant navigation, group-following robot formations, and dynamic tracking position adjustments in crowded spaces.

Agricultural Robots

Uneven terrain traversability, orchard autonomous navigation, and context-aware adaptive pesticide spraying under varying weather.

Legged Locomotion Robots

Virtual passive dynamic walking, kneed walker modeling, and robust policy learning for bipedal and humanoid locomotion.

Robot Learning

Deep reinforcement learning for multi-robot path planning, continual learning for visual tracking, and sim-to-real transfer.

Vision Language Action (VLA)

Vision-Language Model (VLM) driven interaction, human-following strategies, and synthetic scenario policy learning for humanoid tasks.
