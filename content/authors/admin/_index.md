Cong-Thanh Vu is an **Assistant Professor** in the Department of Mechatronics Engineering at **Hanoi University of Industry (HAUI)**, and the Director of the **Robotics and Autonomous Systems Laboratory (RASL)**.

He received his **Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering** from **National Cheng Kung University (NCKU)**, Tainan, Taiwan in 2026, and his **M.S.** (2021) and **B.S.** (2019) in Mechatronics Engineering from Hanoi University of Industry (HAUI).

### Research Topics
- **Human–Robot Interaction**: Adaptive companionship and proactive assistance.
- **Human-Aware Navigation**: Socially compliant mobile robot navigation and group following.
- **Agricultural Robots**: Uneven terrain traversability and adaptive pesticide spraying.
- **Legged Locomotion Robots**: Passive dynamic walking and humanoid locomotion.
- **Robot Learning**: Deep reinforcement learning and continual learning for tracking.
- **Vision-Language-Action**: Multimodal VLM-driven interaction and zero-shot policy learning.
